About this portal
What it replaces, how a term rolls over, and where the numbers come from.

What it is

  • A single sheet-backed calendar that colour-codes holidays and exam windows and computes working days by formula instead of by hand.
  • Faculty add dates to their own Google Calendar in one click, so a date change never has to be chased by circular.
  • The sheet's last-modified stamp is on the page, so nobody has to wonder whether they are reading an old copy.

One term at a time

  • This document is the Odd Semester calendar. An academic year also has an Even Semester (roughly January to June) published as its own calendar.
  • The portal always shows the live term. The next one is prepared alongside it as a draft and only replaces the live term when an admin publishes it.
  • Publishing archives the outgoing term - nothing is deleted, and past terms stay printable from the console.

Where the numbers come from

  • Each day carries a type code (working, holiday, Sunday, minor exam, feedback, marks display, made-working, induction, exam).
  • Working-day totals, the completion bar and the exam countdown are all counted from those codes - change a code, every number moves.
  • The legend under the calendar is the same list the sheet's colours and the printed report use, so a colour cannot mean two things.

Paperless by design

  • The circular does not need reprinting when a date moves - the link is the document.
  • Reprints avoided are counted on the dashboard, in sheets and in trees.
  • Print only when a signed copy is genuinely required: the Report tab produces the formal, letterheaded version.
Still printable. The Report tab renders the whole term in the institutional format - letterhead, document control, legend and signature block - so a signed hard copy is one Ctrl+P away when it is actually needed.

Prepare the next term

Every week between those dates is created, Sundays are coded S and any date on the Holidays tab is coded H with its occasion written in. You then adjust exam and feedback windows by hand - the tedious half is already done.

Publish · roll the term over

Publishing archives the live term, promotes the draft into its place and raises a release banner on the portal. Sheet names never change, so nothing downstream breaks. An academic year is two of these: Odd, then Even.
Archived terms stay in the workbook as Archive_<year>_<term> and remain printable.
